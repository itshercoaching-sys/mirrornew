The Mirror Theory

You are not the reflection you were handed.

Think of a hall of mirrors. Every mirror shows you a version of yourself, stretched, shrunk or twisted, and it is hard to know which one is true. Growing up, your family, rejection, trauma, culture, teachers and relationships handed you mirrors. Most people mistake the reflection for who they really are.

Experience→Belief→Identity→Behaviour→Reality

This is how one experience quietly becomes the life you are living.

Trigger→Reaction→Revelation→Conviction→Healing→Transformation

This is how a trigger becomes a doorway instead of a wound.

Your brain is a prediction machine. It uses past experience to forecast what comes next and notices what fits. A belief formed early can quietly steer what you see for decades, until someone holds up a mirror that shows you the difference.

I will also ask you something most people avoid: where have you smashed or avoided your own mirrors? This is not about blame. It is about taking your power back.
